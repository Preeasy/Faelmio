Our Crunchy 3-Color Squishy Ball is a popular sensory fidget toy that delivers a uniquely satisfying experience — a crunchy TPR outer shell with wax coating that creates ASMR crackle sounds when squeezed, wrapping soft clay inside for a smooth, slow-rebound tactile feel. Compact and portable, it fits easily in a pocket, pencil case or desk drawer for on-the-go stress relief.

Why buyers love this crunchy squishy ball#

  • 3-color marbled appearance — visually eye-catching with pink, yellow and blue layered in a swirled pattern
  • Dual sensory experience — crunchy textured outer layer with soft clay filling inside
  • ASMR sound effect — satisfying crispy crunch sounds when you squeeze and knead it
  • Great stress relief — helps release anxiety and calm down in the office, classroom or at home
  • Certified safe — EN71, ASTM F963-23, CE and CPC compliant, tested by DTI / NBTS

What makes the “crunchy” texture special#

Unlike ordinary squishy toys that are uniformly soft, this fidget ball combines a brittle wax-coated TPR outer shell with a soft clay inner filling. When you squeeze it, the first moment produces a gentle crispy crackle — that’s the wax shell breaking sound — before the soft clay gives way. This two-stage feel is what makes it popular with sensory-seekers, ADHD focus tools and stress-relief buyers.

Portable stress relief for all ages#

At 7 cm diameter the squishy ball fits a pocket, pencil case or desk drawer. It is a quiet, screen-free fidget for classrooms, offices, travel and therapy settings. The crunchy ASMR element adds an auditory layer that many sensory-toy users find calming. Recommended for ages 3+ with adult supervision for younger children.

Retail-ready packaging and wholesale logistics#

Each set contains 12 assorted marbled-color balls in a printed color box with PVC inner box. A master carton holds 24 boxes (288 pcs), measures 62.5 × 47.5 × 39.5 cm, weighs 21 kg and occupies 0.117 m³ — about 68,900 pcs per 20GP, 167,000 pcs per 40HQ container.

Compliance and testing#

Tested by accredited third-party labs (DTI / NBTS) to EN 71-1/-2/-3 and ASTM F963-23, CE and CPC compliant. Recommended for ages 3+. Choking-hazard warning is included on retail packaging.

Buyer questions

What is inside this squishy ball?

+

It is filled with soft clay, matched with a crunchy TPR outer shell for a special tactile feeling and ASMR crunch sound.

Is this fidget toy suitable for kids?

+

It is recommended for kids aged 6+. Please avoid tearing it open and eating the inner clay filling. Choking hazard — not for children under 3.

Does the crunchy texture change over time?

+

The outer TPR shell is tear-resistant and durable. The crunchy wax coating may wear gradually after very heavy repeated use — this is normal and part of the sensory experience.

What is the price per set?

+

EXW pricing is USD 9.49 per set for 1–100 sets, USD 9.45 for 100–500 sets, and USD 9.35 for 500+ sets. Each set contains 12 pieces.

What is the minimum order quantity?

+

MOQ is 1 set (12 pcs) for stock items. Custom colors start at 1,000 pcs; custom packaging starts at 3,000 pcs.

How fast can you ship?

+

Stock items ship within 7 days. Custom orders take 15–20 days. We quote EXW, FOB, CIF and DDP terms.

What certifications do you have?

+

Tested to EN71-1/-2/-3 and ASTM F963-23 by accredited third-party labs (DTI / NBTS), CE and CPC compliant.

Can I order a sample?

+

Yes. Stock colours ship as single-set samples (12 pcs) within 7 days. Custom orders require a 1,000 pcs production run.
