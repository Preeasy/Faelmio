A crispy, potato-shaped squishy fidget toy that delivers a uniquely satisfying sensory experience — a brittle wax-coated shell that crackles under pressure, wrapping a tear-resistant PU foam core that slowly springs back every time. It is designed for fidget collectors, kids 3+, teens and adults who want quiet stress relief, focus support or a fun desk companion.

Why buyers love this crispy potato squishy#

  • Crunchy wax shell — a one-of-a-kind texture that pops and crackles when squeezed, instantly different from ordinary soft squishies
  • Slow-rebound PU core — tear-resistant foam bounces back after every squeeze, built for repeated daily use
  • Cute potato face with chocolate-drip — assorted expressions, ideal for impulse buys, party favors and collector shelves
  • Retail-ready color box — each potato is individually boxed with insert foam; 12 pcs per display box, shelf-ready for supermarkets, gift shops and online listings
  • Certified safe — EN71, ASTM F963-23, CE and CPC compliant, tested by DTI / NBTS for global retail

What makes the “crispy” texture different#

Most squishy toys are uniformly soft. This crispy fidget toy adds a thin wax coating over a PU foam core, so the first squeeze produces a gentle crackle before the slow-rebound foam takes over. That two-stage feel — crisp then soft — is why the range performs well with sensory-seekers, ADHD focus tools and stress-relief buyers.

Sensory and stress relief for kids, teens and adults#

At 95 × 50 × 60 mm the potato fits a pocket, pencil case or desk drawer. It is a quiet, screen-free fidget for classrooms, offices, travel and therapy settings. The wax crackle adds an auditory element that many sensory-toy users find calming, while the slow rebound supports repetitive, low-effort squeezing.

Buyer questions

Is the crispy squishy potato safe for kids?

+

Yes. Tested to EN71-1/-2/-3 and ASTM F963-23 by accredited third-party labs (DTI / NBTS), and CE and CPC compliant. Recommended age is 3+ because the set contains small parts.

What does 'crispy' feel like?

+

The outer wax coating is slightly brittle, so it crackles and pops when you squeeze it, while the PU foam core slowly rebounds. The wax skin may wear gradually after repeated squeezing — that is normal and part of the sensory experience.

What is the price per set?

+

FOB pricing is USD 13.59 per set for 1–100 sets, USD 13.49 for 100–500 sets, and USD 13.39 for 500+ sets. Each set contains 12 pieces.

What is the minimum order quantity?

+

MOQ is 1 set (12 pcs) for stock items. Custom colors, scents or faces start at 1,000 pcs; custom packaging starts at 3,000 pcs.

How many pieces fit in a carton?

+

One master carton holds 12 color boxes × 12 pcs = 144 pcs, measures 82 × 56.5 × 41 cm, weighs 19 kg and occupies 0.19 m³. A 40HQ container loads about 51,552 pcs.

How fast can you ship?

+

Stock items ship within 7 days. Custom orders take 15–20 days. We quote FOB, CIF and DDP terms, and can consolidate with other fidget toys in one shipment.

What is a crispy squishy toy?

+

A crispy squishy is a sensory fidget made of a thin wax coating over a slow-rebound PU foam core. The wax shell crackles and pops when squeezed, then the foam slowly springs back — giving both sound and touch feedback.

Can I order a sample before bulk production?

+

Yes. Stock colours ship as single-set samples (12 pcs) within 7 days; custom colours or faces require a 1,000 pcs production run.
