Sensory & stress relief#

The crispy fidget toy is a go-to tool for anxiety relief, ADHD focus support and sensory play. Its small size (95 mm) fits in a pocket, pencil case or desk drawer — ideal for classrooms, offices, travel and therapy settings.

Before you order

Buyer questions

Is the crispy fidget toy safe for kids?

+

Yes. It is tested to EN71-1/-2/-3 and ASTM F963-23 by accredited labs (DTI / NBTS), CE and CPC compliant. Recommended age 3+ due to small parts.

What does 'crispy' feel like?

+

The outer wax coating gives a satisfying crunchy texture when squeezed, while the tear-resistant PU foam inner core bounces back. The brittle skin may wear after repeated squeezing — this is normal and part of the sensory experience.

What is the minimum order quantity?

+

MOQ is 1 set (12 pcs) for stock items. Customized products require 1,000 pcs, custom packaging 3,000 pcs.

How fast can you ship?

+

Stock items ship within 7 days. Custom orders take 15–20 days. We offer EXW, FOB, CIF and DDP terms.

Can I customize the logo and packaging?

+

Yes. Logo print or emboss on the egg, fully custom color box, hang tags and FBA labels available. NDA available on request.
