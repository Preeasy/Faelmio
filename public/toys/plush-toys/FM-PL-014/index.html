Product overview#

This is a made-to-order plush bear, not a stock item. You choose the size, fabric, colour and branding, and we handle pattern making, sampling, compliance testing and bulk production with a plush factory we have worked with for over eight years.

The bear is built with embroidered eyes and nose instead of plastic safety eyes, which removes the small-parts risk and makes the 0+ age grading straightforward. Seams are double-stitched and pull-tested to 4 kg, the most common failure point in cheap plush.

Typical buyers are gift shops, museum and theme-park stores, promotional agencies and Amazon sellers launching a private-label soft toy.

Materials and safety#

  • Outer fabric — short-pile plush velvet, AZO-free and colourfast to washing. Minky, fleece and organic cotton are available on request.
  • Filling — 100% PP cotton, hypoallergenic and OEKO-TEX certified.
  • Decoration — polyester embroidery thread, colour-matched to your Pantone reference.
  • Compliance — EN71-1/2/3 (EU), ASTM F963 and CPSIA (US), plus GPSR documentation for European fulfilment.
  • Lab reports — issued by SGS, Intertek or BV per order, not recycled from an old batch.

How an order runs#

  1. Brief — send a sketch, artwork or reference sample with target size, quantity and destination market.
  2. Quote — you receive unit price by tier, sample cost and tooling cost within 24 hours.
  3. Sample — a physical pre-production sample in 7–10 days, couriered to you for approval.
  4. Production — 25–40 days after approval, with photo updates at cutting, stuffing and packing.
  5. Inspection — AQL Level II pre-shipment inspection before the cartons are sealed.
  6. Shipping — FOB Ningbo, or DDP door-to-door to the EU, US, Latin America and Japan.

Why buyers consolidate here#

Most of our clients order plush alongside pet toys, fidget ranges or educational sets. We hold goods in our Yiwu warehouse for 15 days free of charge, so several suppliers can be combined into one container and one set of export documents. That is usually where the largest landed-cost saving comes from — not from the unit price.

Before you order

Buyer questions

What is the MOQ for a custom plush bear?

+

500 pieces per design for a new plush bear with your own colour and embroidery. Stock shapes with only a hangtag change start at 200 pieces. Below 500 pieces we can quote from existing moulds but cannot change the pattern.

How long does sampling and production take?

+

A physical sample is made in 7–10 days after we confirm artwork and fabric. Once you approve it, mass production runs 25–40 days depending on size mix and quantity. Total door-to-door to Europe or the US is typically 8–11 weeks.

Which safety tests are included?

+

Every order is tested to EN71-1/2/3 for the EU and ASTM F963 plus CPSIA for the US. Reports are issued by a third-party lab (SGS, Intertek or BV) and shared before shipment. Flammability and AZO tests are added for textile-heavy designs.

Can you match a plush I already sell?

+

Yes. Send a physical sample or clear photos with dimensions and we will reverse-engineer the pattern, then quote tooling separately. Most re-development projects need one pattern revision cycle.

What does shipping cost for 500 pieces?

+

500 bears at 30 cm fill roughly 9 cartons (0.76 m³, 86 kg). Sea LCL to the US West Coast is around $180–260, air freight around $450–600, and express courier around $700–900. Use the freight estimator above for your own quantity and destination.

Can I order mixed colours and sizes in one order?

+

Yes. Colour and size can be mixed inside the MOQ as long as each colourway reaches 150 pieces. Cartons can be packed as solid colour or as an assortment, whichever suits your retail or FBA setup.
