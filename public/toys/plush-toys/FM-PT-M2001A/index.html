Why horror fans love this N plush#

  • Character-accurate design — the signature white mask face, dripping mask line and messy spiky black hair are reproduced with fine embroidery, instantly recognizable to any Mandela Catalogue viewer.
  • Premium short plush + PP cotton — high-elastic filling keeps the doll’s sitting posture without collapsing, fluffy and skin-friendly to the touch.
  • Tight, lint-resistant stitching — clean seams that survive hugging, handling and long-term shelf display.
  • Stable sitting pose — designed to sit upright on desks and shelves without propping, ideal for collection displays.
  • Gift-ready for horror fans — a safe-bet present for birthdays, holidays and fan meetups; creepy outside, soft inside.

Character details that sell the doll#

The N character lives in the details: the white mask panel is embroidered rather than printed, so it will never crack or fade; the single wide eye and grinning mouth are stitched with tight satin embroidery; and the spiky hair uses long-pile plush that can be finger-styled. The body, arms and feet use a denser short plush so the doll keeps its shape at 175 g.

Buyer questions

What size is this Mandela Catalogue N plush?

+

It stands 250 mm tall and 230 mm wide (25 × 23 cm) and weighs 175 g — a standard collectible plush size that is easy to display on a desk or shelf and easy to carry to fan meetups.

Is the plush safe and easy to clean?

+

Made of premium plush fabric with embroidered details, surface wipe-clean is recommended. Do not soak or machine wash, to protect the embroidered mask pattern and the spiky hair texture.

What age is this plush suitable for?

+

Recommended for ages 3 and up. It carries a small-parts choking-hazard warning, so it is not intended for children under 3 years.

How is each doll packed?

+

Each plush is individually sealed in a polybag, then packed in a 5-ply export carton of 12 pcs. Retail-ready packaging, custom color boxes and Amazon FBA labels are available on request.

What certifications does this plush toy have?

+

The toy is tested to EN 71-1/-2/-3 and ASTM F963-23 by third-party labs (DTI / NBTS), and CE and CPC documentation is available for EU and US import compliance.
