Before you order

Buyer questions

How thick is the vinyl on this pool float?

+

The float uses 0.3 mm+ thick UV-resistant vinyl, which is the specification stated for this product. The thicker film is what supports the reinforced seam construction and repeated seasonal use.

What shapes can I order?

+

Dozens of shapes are available, including unicorns, pizza slices, donuts and flamingos, plus custom designs developed from your artwork or sample.

How is the float inflated and deflated?

+

Each float uses safety valves for easy inflation and deflation, so it can be packed flat for retail and inflated at the point of sale or at home.

What is the minimum order quantity?

+

MOQ is 200 pcs. Custom shapes, colours and full-colour printing can be quoted with your artwork, and the mould fee is quoted on request.

How many floats fit in a container?

+

A master carton holds 50 pcs at 70 × 50 × 40 cm, 13 kg and 0.14 m³. That works out to about 10,000 pcs per 20GP and 24,250 pcs per 40HQ at standard loading, rounded down to whole cartons.

Are the seams strong enough for adult use?

+

The seams are reinforced and the existing product specification lists them as tested to 100 kg. We quote exactly that figure and do not claim any additional load rating.
