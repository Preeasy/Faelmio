Before you order

Buyer questions

What age group is this building blocks set for?

+

It is designed for preschool STEM play, supporting fine motor skills, spatial reasoning and creativity. The exact age labelling is confirmed with your order requirements and the applicable CE, EN71, ASTM and CPSIA compliance for your market.

Is the ABS plastic safe for children?

+

The set is made from safe ABS plastic with smooth edges and no sharp parts, and is listed as CE, EN71, ASTM and CPSIA compliant. No additional test reports are claimed beyond those certifications.

Are the bricks compatible with other brands?

+

Yes. The blocks are designed to be compatible with major brick brands, so they can be mixed into existing collections and expanded with standard bricks.

What is the minimum order quantity?

+

MOQ is 300 sets. Multiple sizes, piece counts and colour combinations are available, and custom packaging or branding can be quoted with your artwork; the mould fee is quoted on request.

How many sets fit in a container?

+

A master carton holds 60 pcs at 58 × 42 × 48 cm, 19 kg and 0.117 m³. On the standard loading estimate that is about 14,340 pcs per 20GP and 34,860 pcs per 40HQ, rounded down to whole cartons.

Can I order custom packaging with my brand?

+

Yes. Custom printed color boxes or carry cases, printed building guides and Amazon FBA labels are available, with the packaging mould fee quoted on request.
