The process

How an OEM project runs

Six checkpoints, each with a deliverable you sign off before we move on.

01

Concept & 3D design

Your sketch, reference or mood board becomes a manufacturable 3D file.

02

Prototype sample

Hand-made or 3D-printed sample for form, size and safety review.

03

Tooling & moulds

Steel or soft moulds built in partner tool rooms, with revision tracking.

04

First article inspection

Dimensional, material and safety checks before the production run.

05

Pre-production run

100–500 pcs pilot batch for your final approval.

06

Mass production & DDP

Bulk run, AQL Level II inspection, consolidation and door-to-door delivery.
