## What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, business email, company, country | When you submit an enquiry form | To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation |
| Phone / WhatsApp number | When you provide it | To follow up on an active enquiry |
| Product interest, target quantity, destination | When you submit a form | To quote accurately and advise on compliance |
| Message content and attachments | When you contact us | To understand and answer your request |
| Technical data — IP address, browser type, pages visited | Automatically on every visit | Security, abuse prevention and aggregate traffic statistics |

We do not collect payment card details on this website. Payments are arranged by invoice and bank transfer or through third-party payment platforms.

## How we use it

- Responding to enquiries and preparing quotations
- Fulfilling orders and arranging shipping and compliance documents
- Answering after-sales questions and handling claims
- Improving the website and understanding which pages are useful
- Meeting legal, tax and customs obligations

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## Cookies and analytics

This site uses only what is necessary to function, plus aggregate traffic measurement. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect site functionality, but the site remains readable.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion
- Object to or restrict processing
- Receive your data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these, email [maxon@maxoncn.com](mailto:maxon@maxoncn.com). We respond within 30 days.

**California residents (CCPA/CPRA):** you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.
