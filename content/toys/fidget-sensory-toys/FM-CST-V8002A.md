A crispy, tomato-shaped squishy fidget toy that delivers a uniquely satisfying sensory experience — a brittle wax-coated shell that crackles under pressure, wrapping a tear-resistant PU foam core that slowly springs back every time. It is designed for fidget collectors, kids 3+, teens and adults who want quiet stress relief, focus support or a fun desk companion.

## Why buyers love this crispy tomato squishy

- **Crunchy wax shell** — a one-of-a-kind texture that pops and crackles when squeezed, instantly different from ordinary soft squishies
- **Slow-rebound PU core** — tear-resistant foam bounces back after every squeeze, built for repeated daily use
- **Cute cartoon tomato face** — assorted pink and green expressions, ideal for impulse buys, party favors and collector shelves
- **Retail-ready color box** — each tomato is individually boxed with insert foam; 12 pcs per display box, shelf-ready for supermarkets, gift shops and online listings
- **Certified safe** — EN71, ASTM F963-23, CE and CPC compliant, tested by DTI / NBTS for global retail

## What makes the "crispy" texture different

Most squishy toys are uniformly soft. This crispy fidget toy adds a thin wax coating over a PU foam core, so the first squeeze produces a gentle crackle before the slow-rebound foam takes over. That two-stage feel — crisp then soft — is why the range performs well with sensory-seekers, ADHD focus tools and stress-relief buyers.

|---|---|---|
| Outer feel | Brittle wax shell, crackles | Smooth, uniformly soft |
| Rebound | Slow, controlled | Fast |

| Best for | Fidget collectors, sensory seekers | General desk toys |

## Sensory and stress relief for kids, teens and adults

At 8 × 8 cm the tomato fits a pocket, pencil case or desk drawer. It is a quiet, screen-free fidget for classrooms, offices, travel and therapy settings. The wax crackle adds an auditory element that many sensory-toy users find calming, while the slow rebound supports repetitive, low-effort squeezing.
