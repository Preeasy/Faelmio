Soft corn fleece squeaky plush dog chew toy with a built-in squeaker — gently cleans teeth, relieves boredom and anxiety, and is suitable for puppies and all-sized dogs. Available in three cute animal designs: elephant, rabbit and parrot.

## Why pet owners love this squeaky chew toy

- **Corn fleece material** — soft yet durable, gentle on dog's gums and won't hurt puppy teeth during teething
- **Built-in squeaker** — attracts your dog's attention instantly, turning chewing into interactive play
- **Dental cleaning action** — the bumpy fleece surface scrapes away plaque and massages gums while your dog chews
- **Reinforced stitching** — bite-resistant seams hold up to aggressive chewers, extend the toy's life
- **Boredom & anxiety relief** — perfect for puppies left alone, crate training, or dogs that need a calm chew companion

## Corn fleece vs regular plush — what makes this toy different

Most stuffed dog toys use regular plush fabric that feels flat and wears quickly. Corn fleece has a textured, waffle-like surface that provides a gentle massaging action on your dog's gums while they chew, plus it's more tear-resistant than standard plush.

| Feature | Corn fleece squeaky toy | Regular plush dog toy |
|---|---|---|
| Surface feel | Bumpy, waffle-textured | Smooth, flat |
| Dental benefit | Gently scrapes plaque | Minimal |
| Durability | Tear-resistant stitching | Average |
| Squeaker quality | Built-in, long-lasting | Often fragile |
| Best for | Teething puppies, heavy chewers | Light play, cuddling |

## Three animal designs for every dog personality

Choose the style that matches your pup's vibe:

- **Elephant** — grey corn fleece, big floppy ears, gentle giant vibe — great for calm dogs that like long chew sessions
- **Rabbit** — pink corn fleece, long ears, bouncy personality — perfect for playful puppies and energetic breeds
- **Parrot** — colourful red/blue/yellow corn fleece, beak detail — stands out in any toy box, ideal for training rewards

All three variants share the same durable construction and squeaker mechanism.

## Dental cleaning & boredom relief — for puppies and adult dogs

At 260 × 340 mm this toy is the right size for small, medium and large dogs. The soft corn fleece texture is gentle enough for teething puppies (4–6 months) who need to soothe sore gums, while the reinforced stitching stands up to adult dogs with stronger jaws.

Leave one in your puppy's crate for calm alone time, or toss it during fetch — the built-in squeaker makes every play session feel new. Veterinarians recommend chew toys like this to redirect dogs from destructive chewing on furniture or shoes.
