## Service Items
1. Supplier sourcing & factory verification
2. Price negotiation & full order follow-up
3. Pre-shipment inspection (AQL Level II)
4. Factory on-site QC fee: $350/day (Guangdong), $250/day (Zhejiang)
5. Goods consolidation, 15 days free warehouse storage
6. OEM project management & sample tracking
7. Global shipping & DDP door-to-door delivery

## Service Fee
- Normal bulk purchase: **5% – 8%** of total goods value
- OEM custom toy order: **8% – 12%** of goods value
- Wig products (cross category): fixed **5%** service fee
- Small order handling fee: flat **$50** per order
- Monthly minimum service charge: **$100**
- Sample cost: **$50** per sample

## Compliance Support
Toys certification: CE, EN71, ASTM F963, GPSR for EU market.
