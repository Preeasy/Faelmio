OEM toy development requires precise sampling and mold management. We coordinate the entire process from your initial concept to mass production.

## Prototype → Tooling → Pre-production
1. **Concept & 3D rendering** — our design team creates detailed 3D files
2. **Prototype sample** — hand-made or 3D-printed for form validation
3. **Tooling development** — steel molds in partner tool-making facilities
4. **First article inspection** — dimensional check, material test, safety check
5. **Pre-production run** — 100–500 pcs for final approval

## Revisions
Most OEM projects require 2–5 rounds of revisions. We track every change, coordinate mold modifications and ship revision samples to you promptly.
