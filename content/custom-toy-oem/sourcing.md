Finding the right supplier is the most critical step in toy sourcing. Our on-the-ground team maintains direct relationships with 300+ verified toy factories across Zhejiang and Guangdong.

## How It Works
1. You share your product specifications, target MOQ and budget
2. We shortlist 3–5 qualified factories within 48 hours
3. On-site factory audit (license, production line, QC room)
4. Price negotiation, MOQ flexibility and payment terms
5. Sample development and first article approval

## What We Provide
- Factory verification with video evidence
- Transparent quotation breakdown (material, labor, tooling, packaging)
- MOQ reduction negotiation (many factories reduce 30–50% with our leverage)
- Sample coordination and shipping to your office
