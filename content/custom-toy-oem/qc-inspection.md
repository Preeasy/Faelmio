Quality control is non-negotiable when importing toys from China. We offer two inspection modes to protect your brand.

## AQL Level II
Standard pre-shipment inspection covering:
- Visual appearance and workmanship
- Dimensional tolerance checks
- Material safety spot checks
- Packaging compliance
- Function testing

## On-site Factory QC
For high-value or complex orders, our inspectors stay on-site during production:
- First-off inspection at line setup
- In-process checks every 2–4 hours
- Final audit before packing
- Video reports with timestamped evidence

## Reporting
All inspections produce a full PDF report with photos, defect grading and pass/fail determination — sent to you within 24 hours of completion.
