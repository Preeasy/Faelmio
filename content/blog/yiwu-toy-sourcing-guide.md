Yiwu has been the world's wholesale toy capital for over two decades. Every week, thousands of containers of toys leave this small Zhejiang city bound for Amazon warehouses, retail chains and independent brands across the globe. If you're looking to source toys from China, Yiwu should be at the top of your list — but only if you know how to navigate its ecosystem.

## Why Yiwu
Yiwu's strength lies in its concentration. The Yiwu International Trade City (the world's largest wholesale market) houses over 75,000 booths, including more than 10,000 dedicated toy suppliers. Beyond the market, a network of 2,000+ toy factories within a 200km radius offers OEM and custom manufacturing at volumes and prices no other region can match.

Unlike Shenzhen or Dongguan — which focus on electronics and high-tech manufacturing — Yiwu specializes in consumer goods with shorter production cycles and lower minimum order quantities. For most toy categories, you can start as low as 500 pieces for stock items and 1,000–3,000 for custom OEM projects.

## Key Toy Categories
Yiwu's toy suppliers cover every category imaginable:
- **Plush & stuffed toys** — the largest category, with 300+ factories producing everything from keychain-sized mascots to life-size promotional figures
- **Educational & STEM toys** — building blocks, science kits and early learning products, many with CE/EN71 pre-certification
- **Fidget & sensory toys** — the fastest-growing segment since 2021, with push-pop bubbles, stress toys and ADHD aids
- **RC & electronic toys** — budget 1:24 scale cars, drones and light-up novelty items
- **Inflatable & outdoor toys** — pool floats, beach balls and promotional inflatables using 0.3mm+ thick vinyl

## How to Find Suppliers
There are three main paths:

**Visit the trade market.** Phase 1 of Yiwu International Trade City has the largest concentration of toy booths. Bring a translator, business cards and a USB drive for exchanging factory catalogs. Most booths represent 2–5 factories, so ask about direct factory visits before committing.

**Use B2B platforms.** Alibaba.com and Made-in-China.com have thousands of Yiwu-listed toy suppliers. Filter by "Verified Gold Supplier" and request factory audit reports before ordering samples. Be wary of suppliers with generic product photos and no company location details.

**Work with a local agent.** A reliable sourcing agent based in Yiwu will save you months of trial and error. They maintain direct factory relationships, can negotiate 30–50% lower MOQs, and handle on-site QC during production. Most agents charge 5–8% commission with no hidden fees.

## Avoiding Common Pitfalls
The biggest mistake new buyers make is ordering blind. Always:
1. **Request and verify factory licenses.** Business license, ISO 9001, and toy-specific safety certifications should be verifiable through government databases.
2. **Order samples before bulk.** Even small 50-piece test orders reveal quality issues, packaging problems and shipping delays.
3. **Inspect before shipment.** AQL Level II pre-shipment inspection catches the defects that will anger your customers.
4. **Clarify Incoterms.** FOB Yiwu is standard; beware suppliers quoting EXW who expect you to handle local transport and export customs.
