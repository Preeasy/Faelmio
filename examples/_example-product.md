## Product Overview
100% baby-safe plush bear with embroidered eyes and nose. Hypoallergenic stuffing, machine-washable fabric.

## Specifications
- Sizes: 20cm · 30cm · 45cm
- Filling: PP cotton, 100% hypoallergenic
- Cover: Super-soft short-plush velvet
- Weight: 120g – 380g per piece

## Customization
- Colors: 6 stock colors, custom pantone on MOQ ≥ 3,000 pcs
- Logo: Embroidery (recommended) or sublimation print
- Packaging: Opp bag / gift box / polybag with header card

## Packaging & Shipping
- Carton size: 60 × 40 × 35 cm (mixed sizes)
- 20GP container capacity: ~18,000 pcs (30cm size)

## Compliance
- CE · EN71-1/2/3 · ASTM F963 · CPSIA · AZO-free fabric
